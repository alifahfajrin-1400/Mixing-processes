Ideal doesn't mean nothing changes.

Quick question: If ΔV = 0, must the mixture be ideal? No. Other excess properties may remain nonzero. A single zero volume change does not establish ideal-solution behavior.

What happens at a pure-component limit?

x₁ → 0 or x₁ → 1
ME → 0

In a pure-component limit, there is no binary mixing. Common correlations include a factor that vanishes at both ends:

ME = x₁x₂ f(x₁)
Optional advanced material · Partial molar excess properties

At fixed temperature and pressure for a binary mixture, Eqs. 11.14–11.15 relate the mixture's molar excess property and its composition derivative to the two partial molar excess properties:

M̄₁E = ME + (1 − x₁) dME/dx₁
M̄₂E = ME − x₁ dME/dx₁

The textbook's subsequent worked example is intentionally excluded.
